About Lahema Labour Consultancy

Messrs Lahema Labour Consultancy Limited is a Ndola-based practice focused exclusively on labour and employment law, working with employers and employees across the Copperbelt on disputes, contracts, compliance and workplace policy.

Our focus

Unlike a general legal practice, Lahema works only on labour and employment matters — employment disputes, contract drafting, disciplinary hearings, retrenchment, workplace policy and compliance, and labour relations. That focus means every matter is handled with depth in Zambian labour law.

Where we're based

Our office is at the Development Bank of Zambia building, 2nd Floor, Room 1 & 2, Right Wing, between Kafubu Mall and the ZRA Building in Ndola — accessible to employers and employees across the Copperbelt.
